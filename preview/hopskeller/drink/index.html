American Innovations

Raspberry Ale

6.0% ABV9 IBU

Our flagship wheat ale is aged on raspberries after fermentation for a sweet-tart finish and a hint of berry aroma.

Learn more about Raspberry Ale

Our “flagship beer” and an easy drinker! Aged on raspberries after fermentation, this wheat beer is sweet but tart, with just a hint of raspberry flavor and aroma. Best enjoyed on its own.

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Paradise City, Tangled Up in Blue, or a pizza with pineapple or spice

White Rock Wheat Ale

4.2% ABV5 IBU

A light, bready, tart wheat ale honoring the White Rock Nature Preserve. Try it with a splash of house fruit syrup or as a shandy.

Learn more about White Rock Wheat Ale

Crisp, bready, tart, and light as a feather, White Rock is our homage to Clifftop, The Conservation Fund, and the Illinois Audubon Society’s 2026 acquisition of 1,000 acres to the White Rock Nature Preserve. Although not a true Berliner Weisse by style, this unique beer shares its cousin brew’s love of syrup additions. Try it by itself for a clean easy-drinker; spice it up with one of our in-house fruit syrups, or turn it into a dynamite shandy with some lemonade or orange soda!

Matt Schweizer

Bluegrass Breeze American Wheat Ale

6.0% ABV20 IBU

A malt-forward wheat ale with bright currant aroma and notes of caramel and biscuit.

Learn more about Bluegrass Breeze American Wheat Ale

Perfect for day-drinking during any season! With a bright and fruity currant-like aroma and a mouth-watering combination of bready, caramel, and biscuit flavors, this malt-forward ale is a fun and easy-drinking beer.

Matt Schweizer
  • Salads pairing: Fresh salads
  • Pub bites pairing: Crab cakes or shrimp skewers

High Desert Pale Ale

5.3% ABV22 IBU

Oktoberfest grains and hops meet a Northwestern pale ale. Smooth and malt-forward with a dry finish.

Learn more about High Desert Pale Ale

A smooth, malt-forward beer that takes the grain bill and hop profile of an Oktoberfest and is reinterpreted as a Northwestern American pale ale. An easy-drinking crowd pleaser with a unique malt profile and a somewhat dry finish.

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Fat Bottomed Girls or Born To Be Wild

Zero New England IPA

7.0% ABV0 IBU

Hazy and heavy on tropical hop aroma, with grapefruit, stone fruit, and mango. Brewed for very little bitterness.

Learn more about Zero New England IPA

A massively-hopped ale featuring none of the bitterness typically associated with hops—so little, in fact, that the beer has no IBUs to its name! This hazy, straw-colored beer greets you with an intense burst of tropical aromas from El Dorado, Mosaic, and Amarillo hops that call to mind grapefruit, stone fruit, tangerines, mango, and pineapple.

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Dixie Chicken or another spicy pizza
  • Salads pairing: Fresh salads
  • Pub bites pairing: Bread bites

Across the Pond IPA

7.6% ABV55 IBU

English malt and balance meet citrusy, piney Pacific Northwest hops. Lively and crisp.

Learn more about Across the Pond IPA

This IPA merges the best of two styles: the grains, mineral profile and balance of a classic English IPA, and the bold hop flavors of a medley of the “4 Cs” (plus Citra!) classic to IPAs of the American NW. A lively ale featuring the dank resin, citrus and pine notes of Northwestern IPAs, yet crisp enough to make you want another!

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Pizzas with our house sauce and fresh-shredded mozzarella

Maple Pecan Märzen

6.6% ABV18 IBU

A toasty Oktoberfest-style lager brewed with local pecans and extra-dark Vermont maple syrup. Contains pecans.

Learn more about Maple Pecan Märzen

Originally made in collaboration with Scott Kurtz (Kurtzhaus Brewing) and Kyle Wolf (Carboy Junkies Homebrew Club), our Maple Pecan Märzen weds the toasty and bready malt flavors of a traditional Oktoberfest lager with 40 lbs. of local-harvested pecans and extra-dark Vermont maple syrup.

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Beast of Burden
  • Salads pairing: Chicken Caesar

British Classics

Hang Glider Light Lager

4.5% ABV8 IBU

English Pearl malt, rice, and English hops make a clear, crisp lager with a light malt finish.

Learn more about Hang Glider Light Lager

Our light lager is made with English Pearl malt, crisp rice, and English hops to create a unique spin on the quintessential lawnmower beer. With a subtle malt backbone and a crystal-clear finish, this crowd-pleaser is just what you need for patio weather!

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Instant Karma or Green-Eyed Lady
  • Pub bites pairing: Pretzels or crab cakes

Market Cross London Lager

5.9% ABV20 IBU

Inspired by the lagers of 1920s London, with heritage English malt, Fuggles, and East Kent Goldings.

Learn more about Market Cross London Lager

Modeled off lagers popular in London in the 1920s, this beer is a unique experience that brings together heritage English malts alongside Fuggles and East Kent Goldings hops. Clean, refreshing, and decidedly English, this all-around easy drinker is perfect for fans of lighter beers and those looking for a lager interpreted in English style.

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Beast of Burden or Island Fever

Ormr’s Pub Ale

4.5% ABV28 IBU

An English-style best bitter with citrusy Vista hops, bready malt, and a dry, crisp finish with a hint of rye sweetness.

Learn more about Ormr’s Pub Ale

In honor of the English town where Matt studied 20 years ago, this beer features all the fun qualities of an English best bitter, along with the citrus hop notes we see in some Cornish ales. This beer also highlights the hops of local 3.9 Hops (Millstadt) and Sugar Creek Malting (Indiana), a farm-to-bag craft maltster. The bready aroma of this ale gives way to a light, flavorful, and refreshing brew. Malt and bread notes are present, but not too intense; this is a dry, crisp beer that finishes clean and with a slight lingering sweetness from the addition of heirloom malted rye. The Vista hops in this ale give it a citrusy character; it’s like drinking a “baby IPA.” Fantastic by itself.

Matt Schweizer
  • Salads pairing: Fresh salads
  • Pub bites pairing: Tenderloin skewers or pretzels with beer cheese

Breath of Heather

3.7% ABV11 IBU

A light-drinking Scottish-style dark ale with caramel, fresh bread, and toasted malt.

Learn more about Breath of Heather

Our first offering of a Scottish-style ale, and we’re thrilled with the result! This dark ale looks mighty, but goes light on the ABV. A malt-forward offering made of some fantastic Scottish malts, this ale features caramel, bread, and toasty notes. This easy-drinker is great year-round; we hope you love it as much as we do!

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Can’t Get Enough, Sweet Child O’ Mine, or Midnight Rambler
  • Salads pairing: Chicken Caesar

Northern English Brown Ale

6.2% ABV26 IBU

Inspired by ales of Northern England’s coalfields, with roasted chocolate, coffee, caramel, and toffee.

Learn more about Northern English Brown Ale

Inspired by ales made around the Great Northern coalfields of England in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, this dark beer features roasted chocolate and coffee flavors with deep caramel and toffee notes. Complex, robust, and the ultimate “food beer.” Winner of the 2018 Post-Dispatch’s “Top Pours” for “Best Darker Beer.”

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Wood-fired pizza
  • Award: 2018 St. Louis Post-Dispatch Top Pours · Best Darker Beer

Moorgate Porter

5.0% ABV22 IBU

A London-style porter with moderate roast, chocolate, and a medium body.

Learn more about Moorgate Porter

Moorgate is our darkest beer on tap—but don’t let the color scare you! Taking cues from the easy-drinking porters popular among the working class of London in the 19th century, Moorgate features the moderate roast and chocolate flavors we expect from a porter, plus a medium body that keeps the overall experience refreshing. Fans of darker beers will appreciate the complexity of the malt bill; it’s also a great introductory beer to the world of dark ales!

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Just What I Needed or Buffalo Soldier
  • Pub bites pairing: Crab cakes or shrimp skewers

Celebration Barleywine

Learn more about Celebration Barleywine

A hearty and malt-forward brew made for our 10-year anniversary, this English-style barleywine is meant for aging. Give it a try over the next several months and into 2028 to see how it evolves!

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Buffalo Soldier
  • Pub bites pairing: Buffalo Bites
Past brews

Previously brewed at Hopskeller. Availability changes as taps rotate.

Creamy Peach Ale

4.6% ABV8 IBU

A hazy, sweet wheat ale brewed with peach, apricot, and lactose. Contains peaches, apricots, and lactose.

Learn more about Creamy Peach Ale

With its unmistakable hazy orange appearance and fruity aroma, our Creamy Peach Ale is a crowd favorite in the summer. Over 80 lbs of peach and 40 lbs of apricot are added to a light American wheat ale, rounded off with lactose for a sweet and medium-bodied summery ale. (allergy warning: contains peaches, apricots, lactose)

Matt Schweizer

Cascadia American Amber Ale

5.8% ABV28 IBU

A Northwest-style amber ale with caramel and bready malt, citrus, and a touch of pine from Cascade hops.

Learn more about Cascadia American Amber Ale

A true homage to life in the American Northwest, Cascadia is a complex and lively American amber ale, hopped generously with late additions of Cascade hops and dry-hopped with Cascade once again. Rich malt flavors provide a caramel and bready background to the citrus and slight pine aromas and flavor of Oregon’s most beloved hop.

Matt Schweizer

Monroe Meadows Honey Ale

6.8% ABV5 IBU

American wheat and heirloom English barley with local Blessed Bee honey, yarrow, and elderflower. Contains wheat, honey, elderflower, and yarrow.

Learn more about Monroe Meadows Honey Ale

Dedicated to the prairies of Monroe County, this refreshing brew contains American wheat and a small dose of heirloom English barley. Drawn from hives just west of town, local honey from The Blessed Bee gives this beer an unmistakable floral and crisp note. Late additions of yarrow and elderflower round out the finish. If you ever wanted to take in the best of our local meadows, this is a great start (allergy warning: contains wheat, honey, elderflower, yarrow).

Matt Schweizer

Spaghetti Western Italian Pils

5.25% ABV32 IBU

A crisp Italian-American pils brewed with lemongrass, basil, and Northern Brewer hops.

Learn more about Spaghetti Western Italian Pils

Every brew makes its own tune, and our Spaghetti Western Italian-American Pils is no exception. This is also former assistant brewer Conner McDonald’s first solo recipe, and it’s a great one! Brewed with lemongrass and basil, and dry-hopped with Northern Brewer, expect a rich aroma of basil, mint, and oregano over the prominent malt profile. A sweet maltiness pervades through the initial flavor, alongside flavors of lemon and basil -- the finish is dry and crisp, and the mouthfeel is light enough to bring you back for more. We hope you enjoy drinking it as much as we do bringing it to tap!

Matt Schweizer
  • Pizza pairing: Wood-fired pizza

Waterloo Gold Spiced Ale

5.1% ABV25 IBU

An English golden ale inspired by historic Reading Ale, with pepper, ginger, and citrus notes. Contains grains of paradise and cardamom.

Learn more about Waterloo Gold Spiced Ale

Modeled off the historic Reading Ale style, Waterloo Gold adds a little twist -- replacing coriander for cardamom. This crisp, easy-drinking English golden ale greets you with a spicy aroma; on the flavor, expect the most subtle of black pepper, ginger, and citrus notes. Fans of Belgian ales may enjoy the spicy notes throughout this beer! (allergy warning: contains grains of paradise, cardamom)

Matt Schweizer

MoCo Mocha Coffee Chocolate Milk Stout

7.4% ABV40 IBU

A rich stout with cocoa, lactose, and cold brew from Living Millennially in Maeystown. Contains lactose.

Learn more about MoCo Mocha Coffee Chocolate Milk Stout

A generous portion of milk sugar combined with cocoa and cold brew coffee extract from our friends at Living Millennially in Maeystown makes for a dark, rich beer. Expect coffee on the nose and the front of the taste, and chocolate on the finish.

Matt Schweizer
