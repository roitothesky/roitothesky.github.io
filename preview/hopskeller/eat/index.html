Handmade Wood-Fired Pizzas

All of our wood-fired pizzas proudly feature Bacio whole-milk Mozzarella cheese, shredded fresh off the block and made with high-quality cow’s milk and a “kiss” of buffalo milk for extra creaminess and flavor. Unless otherwise noted, all pizzas come with our homemade pizza sauce, made in-house with only four ingredients: top-quality Californian tomatoes, fresh-picked basil, oregano, and extra-virgin olive oil.
